Privacy Policy

Otium · Last updated 24 October 2026

Otium is a closed beta for Berkeley students. This page explains exactly what the app collects, why it needs it, and how to get rid of it. If anything here is unclear, email us and ask.

What we collect

  • Your email address. Used to sign you in and to confirm you're at Berkeley. We send a six-digit code; we never see or store a password.
  • Your profile. Username, display name, and profile photo if you add one.
  • Your date of birth, only if you choose to give it. It is optional, and its only use is to send you a quest on your birthday.
  • The photos you post, along with any caption, and the time they were taken.
  • Your activity: which quests you complete, your streak, your friends, and the likes and comments you write.
  • Reports and blocks you submit, so we can act on them.
  • Usage data: when you open the app and which actions you take, so we can tell whether the app is working. This is processed by PostHog on our behalf.
  • Technical details attached to feedback you send: app version, iOS version, and device model.
  • A notification token for your device, if you allow notifications.

Location

Some quests happen at a specific place. When you complete one, the app compares your location to the quest's location on your device and keeps only the answer: yes or no.

Your coordinates are never uploaded, never stored, and never shown to anyone — including us. Nobody can use Otium to find out where you are or where you have been.

You can refuse location access. You will simply not be able to complete the quests that require being somewhere specific.

Who can see your posts

Only people you have accepted as friends. Friend requests work both ways: someone has to accept before either of you can see the other's posts. This is enforced by the database itself, not only by the app, so a photo of yours is never sent to a device that isn't allowed it.

If you block someone, neither of you can see the other, and any friendship between you ends.

What we never collect

  • Your precise location.
  • Your contacts or address book.
  • The contents of your photos, captions or comments, for analytics purposes.
  • Anything from other apps, or any advertising identifier.

Who else sees it

We don't sell your data and we don't share it with advertisers. We use two services to run the app: Supabase, which stores the database and your photos, and PostHog, which receives usage events. Notifications are delivered by Apple. That's the full list.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. This is a beta, so data may also be reset or lost as we build — don't treat Otium as the only place anything important lives.

Deleting your account

Email us and we will delete your account, your photos, and everything attached to them. You do not have to give a reason, and we will confirm when it's done.

Age

Otium is for people aged 18 and over. You confirm your age when you create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Questions, or to delete your data: theotiumapp@gmail.com
